How it works

  • You're the teacher. Students toss notes to each other. There are 5 notes in total, and a new one appears every few seconds.
  • Every time a note lands in a student's hands, its count goes up by 1. The number on the little paper in their hand is that count. If any note reaches 10, you lose.
  • Missed throws land on the floor. Walk over before the red ring runs out to confiscate it, or the nearest student picks it up.
  • You can also intercept a note mid-air by walking under it.
  • The pale red circle around you is your stare. A student holding a note inside it panics and throws early, and panicked throws miss more often.
